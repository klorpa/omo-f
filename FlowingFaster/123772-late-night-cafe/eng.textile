The two women obeyed and Nilrem turned both women over his knees and slapped them both three times firmly on their bare butts. "Go back to your shackles unless Savanna needs you for anything!" Phyllis and Claudia were both humiliated and on the verge of crying out loud and ran out his hut and all the way back to their triple bunks in the big wood house. Nilrem carefully soaked up the mess on the floor with a large fiber rag and squeezed as much as he could into a large glass container. 'Everything has a use no matter how gross' as the expression goes. He also warily looked out the window towards the mountains crags for any sign of sky dragons coming out early which can smell fear miles away. They can't see in the daylight, but the really hungry ones might try.
